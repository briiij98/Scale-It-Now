Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Scale It Now ("we", "us") collects when you use this website and the Digital Marketing Fundamentals course, and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Purchase details: your full name and e-mail address as entered at checkout, your Razorpay order and payment IDs and the payment status. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details; Razorpay handles those.
  • Course access and certificate: a secure access link tied to your purchase (we store only an irreversible hash of it, and when it was last used) and your certificate details (name, certificate ID, issue date). The lesson you last opened is remembered only in your own browser.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive the order and payment IDs, amount and status. We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) kept by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

  • To give you access to the course you paid for.
  • To verify payments and prevent fraud.
  • To issue your certificate, including the public verification page, which shows only your name, the course and the issue date for a given certificate ID.
  • To respond to your support requests.

Who processes your data

We use trusted service providers to run the site: Netlify (hosting), Supabase (database), Razorpay (payments) and a video provider for the lesson videos (YouTube, a Google service, using its privacy-enhanced embed mode, or another video host). They process data only to provide their service to us.

Advertising and analytics (Meta Pixel)

Our public pages use the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure our advertising: it records page views, that someone started checkout and that a purchase was completed (course name and price), together with the usual browser and device data and cookies set by Meta. We do not send your e-mail address, your private course link or your payment details to Meta. The Pixel is not loaded on the course lesson pages or on the page that opens a private course link. You can block it with your browser privacy settings or an ad-blocker, and manage ad preferences in your Meta account.

Public certificate verification

Anyone who knows a certificate ID can check it on our verification page, which shows only the name, the course and the issue date.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies on this site. Lesson pages embed a YouTube player, and YouTube/Google may set its own cookies or collect usage data when you play a video; see Google's privacy policy.

Retention and your choices

We keep your purchase and course records for as long as your course access is active and as needed for accounting and legal obligations. You can ask us to correct or delete your personal information by contacting our support team. Some records (such as payment records and an issued certificate's verification entry) may need to be retained.

Children

The course is intended for adults. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian's involvement.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the date above shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about privacy: our support team.
